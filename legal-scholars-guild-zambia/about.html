About LSGZ

A national voice, rooted in student leadership

We bring law students together to learn, lead and contribute to Zambia's legal and civic life.

Our background

For law students, across Zambia

The Legal Scholars' Guild of Zambia is a national representative body for law students at higher education institutions across the country. Founded around 2020 [VERIFY EXACT YEAR], LSGZ is managed entirely by law students, typically in their second and third years, elected from recognised Zambian law faculties.

Through professional networking, leadership development and structured engagement with legal and civic institutions, the Guild helps students build skills and contribute to issues that affect their communities.

Our mission

Developing capable legal leaders

To empower law students through professional networking, leadership development and meaningful engagement with the broader legal and civic community.

Our vision

A stronger legal community

A connected and principled community of law students contributing to justice, inclusive development and the rule of law in Zambia.

What guides us

Core values

Justice

Advancing fairness, rights and equal access to opportunity.

Integrity

Acting with honesty, accountability and respect.

Scholarship

Valuing rigorous study, curiosity and lifelong learning.

Leadership & Service

Leading with humility and serving students and communities.

Our objectives

Turning principles into practice

  • Represent and advocate for law students' welfare and academic interests.
  • Create opportunities for professional networking and mentorship.
  • Strengthen practical legal skills through moots, debates and clinics.
  • Support civic education and constructive participation in law reform.
  • Promote an inclusive legal profession, including pathways for young women.

How we are structured

Run by students, for students

The Guild is led by an elected student executive with representation from recognised law faculties. Executive members are typically second- and third-year law students. Institutional representation and governance details should be confirmed and updated as the Guild's records are formalised.
