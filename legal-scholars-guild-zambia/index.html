The Guild

A voice for the next generation of law

LSGZ is a national representative body for law students across Zambian higher education institutions. Founded around 2020 [VERIFY EXACT YEAR], it is run by law students elected from recognised law faculties.

We connect students with the legal and civic community through student welfare advocacy, civic education, law reform engagement, career mentorship, moot courts, debates, legal clinics and sports.

We work for a fair, inclusive and supportive environment for law students, while encouraging principled leadership, academic freedom and meaningful public service.
