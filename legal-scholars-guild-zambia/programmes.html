Learning, advocacy and service in action

Our activities help law students develop professionally while contributing to student welfare and public life.

01 / Student welfare advocacy

Better conditions for learning

LSGZ works with student bodies, including ZANASU, to raise student welfare issues such as legal protections, academic freedom, inclusive education and living conditions. Student accommodation has been a focus of the Guild's Student Welfare Programme.
